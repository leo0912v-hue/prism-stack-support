Jelly Blocks Privacy Policy

← Home · Support

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Jelly Blocks is a free casual block puzzle game published by Wang Jiapeng. This policy explains what information the current Jelly Blocks package handles, including the third-party services it integrates for advertising, attribution, and analytics.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Jelly Blocks does not collect personal information directly. The app has no account registration, sign-in, user profile, chat, social features, or user-generated content.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, health information, payment information, or government identifiers.

2. Local Gameplay Storage

The app stores a small amount of gameplay preference data locally on your device using the platform's local preferences storage:

  • Highest unlocked campaign level
  • Whether the campaign has been completed
  • Sound on/off preference

This information is not sent to Wang Jiapeng and is not synchronized to a server.

Deleting the app, clearing its app data, or using the operating system's reset controls removes the locally stored data. Because there is no account or cloud profile, the developer cannot restore a deleted local save.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, the app shows the Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt. The prompt asks for permission to use your device identifiers to show personalized ads and measure advertising performance.

  • If you allow tracking, ad networks and attribution partners may link device identifiers to your activity across apps and websites for advertising and measurement.
  • If you decline, the game remains fully playable and ads, when network access is available, are served in non-personalized form.

You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services. Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

AppLovin MAX (advertising)

The app shows interstitial and app-open ads served through the AppLovin MAX mediation platform and its mediated advertising networks. To serve and measure ads, these networks may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier and device-generated identifiers), approximate location derived from IP address, and usage data about ad interactions. With your tracking permission this data may be used for personalized advertising; without it, ads are non-personalized. AppLovin's privacy policy: applovin.com/privacy

AppsFlyer (attribution)

AppsFlyer measures installs and campaign performance using device identifiers and IP-derived data. The SDK starts only after you have been shown the App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline tracking, AppsFlyer is run in anonymized mode. AppsFlyer's privacy policy: appsflyer.com/legal/privacy-policy

GameAnalytics (analytics and remote configuration)

GameAnalytics receives anonymous gameplay events (level starts and completions), anonymized ad-revenue events, and provides the game's remote configuration values. It does not collect your name, contact details, or advertising identifier for cross-app tracking. GameAnalytics' privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy

5. Children

Jelly Blocks is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system and no direct personal-data collection.

Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information to the developer.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Tracking / personalized ads: change your decision in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Local gameplay data: delete the app or clear its app data.
  • Access or deletion requests: because there is no account or server-side profile for players, there is no online profile to access or export. Third-party ad networks process data as described in Section 4 and in their own policies.
