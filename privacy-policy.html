Prism Stack Privacy Policy

← Home · Support

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Prism Stack is a free casual block puzzle game published by Wang Jiapeng. This policy explains what information the current Prism Stack package handles.

1. Information We Collect Directly

Prism Stack does not collect personal information directly. The app has no account registration, sign-in, user profile, chat, social features, or user-generated content.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, health information, payment information, or government identifiers.

2. Local Gameplay Storage

The app stores a small amount of gameplay preference data locally on your device using the platform's local preferences storage:

  • Highest unlocked campaign level
  • Whether the campaign has been completed
  • Sound on/off preference

This information is not sent to Wang Jiapeng and is not synchronized to a server. The current board in an active session is not a cloud save.

Deleting the app, clearing its app data, or using the operating system's reset controls removes the locally stored data. Because there is no account or cloud profile, the developer cannot restore a deleted local save.

3. Third-Party Services and Network Access

The current Prism Stack package does not integrate advertising, attribution, analytics, crash-reporting, authentication, cloud-storage, payment, or remote-configuration services. It does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

The app includes local audio playback for game feedback. Audio assets are bundled with the app and are not uploaded.

If a future version adds a third-party service or a new category of data processing, this policy will be updated before that version is distributed.

4. Children

Prism Stack is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app has no account system and no direct personal-data collection, there is no child profile or public player account.

Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe a child has provided personal information to the developer.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Because the current app does not create an account or transmit personal information to the developer, there is no online profile to access, correct, or export. You can remove locally stored gameplay data by deleting the app or clearing its app data.
