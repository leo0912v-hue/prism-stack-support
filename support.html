About Prism Stack

Prism Stack is an English-first, offline block puzzle game. Drag a prism from the tray onto the 8-by-8 board, complete rows or columns, and work through the campaign objectives. Each level introduces a small tactical twist while keeping the core loop simple: place, clear, and plan the next move.

Quick Answers

How do I play?

Drag a piece from one of the tray slots onto the board. Tap-only placement is intentionally disabled. A placement is accepted when the complete piece fits inside empty board cells. Complete a row or column to clear it and advance the current level objective.

What are the special blocks?

  • Bomb Block clears a 3-by-3 area when its effect is triggered.
  • Ice Block needs two line hits before it disappears.
  • Pulse Core is a reactive relic used by campaign objectives.
  • Ward Block is an indestructible anchor that changes how you route pieces.
  • Conveyor Row shifts to the right after an accepted move.

The game shows a short tutorial the first time a new mechanic appears. Open the level list and choose HOW TO PLAY to review the general rules.

How does progress work?

The highest unlocked campaign level, whether the campaign has been completed, and the sound setting are stored locally on the device. The current board is session-only. Completing a level unlocks the next route; after the campaign is complete, the game can enter Endless mode.

I cannot place a piece. Why?

The piece may not fit at the position under your finger, the selected tray slot may already be empty, or the current level may be complete. Try dragging from the center of the piece and release when the ghost preview sits fully inside the board.

Can I turn off sound?

Yes. Use the sound control in the game header. The preference is saved locally for the next launch.

How do I reset my progress?

Prism Stack has no in-game account or cloud profile. To start over, delete the app and reinstall it, or clear the app's local storage using the operating system's app settings. This permanently removes locally stored campaign progress and sound preference.

Does the game show ads?

Yes. Prism Stack is free and supported by ads. An interstitial ad can appear when you advance to the next level, and an app-open ad can appear when you return to the game after leaving it. Ads never appear in the middle of a move and never cover the level-complete screen. Ads are served by AppLovin MAX and its mediation partners.

What about tracking?

On first launch the game shows the Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt. Allowing it enables personalized ads and attribution measurement; declining keeps the game fully playable with non-personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for the services involved (AppLovin MAX, AppsFlyer, GameAnalytics).

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Gameplay is designed to work fully offline, and no account is required. An internet connection only affects ads (they load when online) and anonymous gameplay analytics.
